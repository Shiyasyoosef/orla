Warranty Policy

This page explains how OrlaTrends handles product quality concerns after purchase.

Product Condition

Items should arrive in the condition shown and described on the product page. Please check your order when it is delivered.

Report an Issue

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, contact us with your order number, photos and a short description of the issue.

Review Process

Our team will review the request and guide you on the next step. Approval may depend on product condition, timing and order details.
