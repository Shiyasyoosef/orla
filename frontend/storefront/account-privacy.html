Privacy Policy

This policy explains how OrlaTrends uses personal information when you create an account and shop with us.

Information we collect

Registration collects your name, email address and optional phone number. These details are stored in our customer database and made available to authorized store administrators for account and order management.

When you save an address or place an order, we collect the delivery and order information you provide, including recipient name, phone number, address and selected products.

How we use your information

We use this information to manage your account, save your addresses, process orders, assist with customer enquiries and protect account access.

Passwords and account security

Firebase Authentication handles your email and password. The store sends a Firebase identity token to its server to authenticate you; it does not save your password in your customer profile. Keep your password private and use the password reset option if you believe someone else has accessed your account. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Cookies and browser storage

Account sessions use browser storage and cookies. Our server records session expiry, browser information and IP address for account security. Saved delivery addresses and orders are collected when you use those services.

Service providers

We use Google's Firebase services for authentication, customer data storage and hosting. Read Firebase's privacy information for details about its processing of data. Depending on the services used, information may be processed outside your country.

Your information and requests

For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion, email info@orlatrends.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some order or security records may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations or resolve disputes.

Policy updates

We will update this page when our data handling changes. This privacy policy does not replace our terms of sale.
